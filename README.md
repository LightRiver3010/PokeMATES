# Project Overview
## PokeMATES - Tropical Tower

### Project Overview:
PokeMATES Tropical Tower is a top-down dungeon crawler game inspired by the Pokemon series. In it, the player enters the Tropical Tower, where they can fight wild Pokemon to get stronger. Advancing through the tower floors, they will find Safari zones where they can catch their own Pokemon. Each floor will have a unique area to explore and fight, a Safari zone, and a PokeMart. At the PokeMart, players can use money earned from battles to purchase items such as Pokeballs to catch Pokemon in the Safari zone, and healing items to support their Pokemon during battles. At the top of the tower, likely around floor ten, there will be a final battle between another final trainer. After the player defeats this final boss, the game will end. 
As for the creation of the game, it will be done using RPG Maker XP, with assistance from Pokemon Essentials. This add-on greatly simplifies the creation process for the entire game, giving us all the tools needed to make the game. To help with this process, there is a YouTube playlist online that includes over a hundred short tutorial videos that each explain how to create certain aspects. From simple qualities such as how to create a world map to more advanced parts like custom Pokemon, the tutorials cover all we will need. 
As for our roles, we have split the work evenly. Sebastian will work on Sound Design, including map music and sound effects, along with the Pokemon Database, including adding any new Pokemon. Travis will handle Battle Interactions, including fights with trainers and bosses, and character design, which consists of new characters, sprites, and more. Jimmy will work designing the overworld map and interactions with wild Pokemon, both in and out of the Safari zone. We will be uploading our work after each block to our GitHub repository, through a process that is outlined, step-by-step.
