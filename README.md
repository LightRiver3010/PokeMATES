# Project Overview
## PokéMATES - Tropical Tower

### Project Overview:
PokéMATES Tropical Tower is a top-down Role-Playing-Game (RPG) inspired heavily by the Pokémon series. Similar to traditional Pokémon games, players are able to collect Pokémon, train their own team, catch new Pokémon, battle other trainers, and explore a new world. The plot of the game is as follows: the player wakes up on a shoreline, unsure of how they arrived. As they explore the island they unexpectedly find themselves on, they are introduced to the three main areas of the game: the Tower, the Town, and the Safari Zone. Through these three zones, the main gameplay loop of the game is established. The Tower is the main way for the player to progress through the game - on each of the three floors of the Tower, they must fight trainers, wild Pokémon, and a final boss. After exploring through the Tower, the player will need to rest their Pokémon in the Town. The Town is home to many Non-Player-Characters (NPCs) for the player to interact with, as well as some side quests, separate from the main storyline. The Town also has a PokéCenter, where players can heal their injured Pokémon. After recovering in the Town, the player will want to catch new Pokémon in the Safari Zone. In the Safari Zone, players can use the money earned from battles in the Tower to catch new Pokémon. Across the three major zones of the Safari, there are many different Pokémon to catch, with each zone having a different type theme. After catching these new Pokémon, the player will report back to the Tower, ready to repeat the loop once again. 

Our team used RPG Maker XP - specifically the Pokémon Essentials add-on - to create the entirety of PokéMATES. With these tools, the game-creation process was simplified, shifting the focus of the project from remembering  methods and syntax of Ruby (the language of RPG Maker XP) to creating trainer battles, maps, wild encounters, and more. An online tutorial series under the YouTube channel "Thundaga" was a great help to this project. On this channel are over a hundred tutorial videos, explaining all aspects of creating a Pokémon game from simple events to complex cutscenes. The team plans on extending thanks to this YouTube channel by reaching out on social media.

As for the roles of the team, the work is divided in a simple fashion. Each team member is responsible for their respective zone: Jimmy works on the Tower, Travis creates the Town, and Sebastian makes the Safari Zone. With this system, the work is split evenly among each member, with all of us learning how to use RPG Maker XP successfully. All of our progress is saved on our GitHub repository, which we upload our files to through GitBash.
